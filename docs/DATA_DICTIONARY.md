# Data and result dictionary

## Raw arrays

Each raw CSV is arranged as `rows × samples`.

- `*_DS.csv`: design variables.
- `*_RS.csv`: spectral responses.
- Multilayers: 200-point transmission spectra over the thesis-documented 300–750 nm interval; generated by transfer-matrix calculations.
- 2×2 metasurface: 551-point reflection spectra over 300–750 nm; normal-incidence Ansys Lumerical FDTD.
- Grating: 400-point reflection spectra over 700–1200 nm; broadband TM-polarized Ansys Lumerical FDTD.

## Geometry outputs

- `global_complexity_full.csv`: native-grid and common-grid covariance measures.
- `global_complexity_equal_n_repeats.csv`: repeated global metrics at equal sample count.
- `global_complexity_equal_n_summary.csv`: summary of equal-sample repeats.
- `nonlinear_id_repeats.csv`: per-repeat local intrinsic-dimension estimates.
- `nonlinear_id_summary.csv`: grouped means, standard deviations, and empirical intervals.
- `smoothing_sensitivity.csv`: Savitzky–Golay sensitivity analysis.
- `analysis_metadata.json`: analysis settings and synthetic estimator check.

Key fields:

- `common_pca99`: number of common-grid principal components explaining at least 99% of variance.
- `common_participation_ratio`: inverse squared spectral-weight concentration.
- `common_effective_rank`: entropy-based effective rank.
- `twonn_mle_mean`: mean TwoNN maximum-likelihood intrinsic dimension across repeats.
- `pca99_twonn_ratio`: common-grid PCA99 divided by TwoNN dimension.

## DNN-summary outputs

- `learning_curve_summary.csv`: mean and sample standard deviation across three seeds at six training sizes.
- `capacity_curve_summary.csv`: mean and sample standard deviation across three seeds at five widths.
- `learning_difficulty_summary.csv`: threshold status and continuous `1 - AUC_logN` outcome.
- `capacity_threshold_summary.csv`: observed or right-censored parameter threshold.
- `geometry_learning_merged.csv`: one row per architecture combining geometry and learning outcomes.
- `geometry_learning_correlations.csv`: Pearson and asymptotic Spearman summaries.

Exact rank-test p values are in `results/robustness/exact_spearman_tests.csv`, not in the asymptotic correlation table.
